Support

Need help with Advent Calendar for the Soul, Julekalender for sjelen, or Avvento per l'Anima? Here are answers to the most common questions.

The app won't open, or freezes

Try closing the app completely and reopening it. Make sure you have the latest version installed from the App Store.

I'm not getting a reminder for today's door

Check that notifications are enabled for the app in your phone's settings (Settings → Notifications → find the app).

I can't save or share the image

Check that the app has permission to access Photos in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Photos).

Other question?

Contact us at frode@sepha-soul.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
