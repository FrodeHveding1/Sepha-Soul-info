Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

"Advent Calendar for the Soul", "Julekalender for sjelen" and "Avvento per l'Anima" (the "App") are developed and published by Sepha Soul. We take your privacy seriously, and this page briefly explains what the App does and does not do with information about you.

What data does the App collect?

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. The App requires no sign-in or user account.

What is stored locally on your phone?

Information about which doors of the calendar you have opened is stored only locally on your own device. This information never leaves your phone, and is never sent to us or any third party.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

The App contains no analytics tools, no tracking, and no advertising.

Purchases and payment

Purchases of the App are handled through Apple's App Store. Payment information is processed solely by Apple, under Apple's own privacy terms. We do not have access to this information.

Contact us

If you have questions about privacy, you can contact us at frode@sepha-soul.com.
